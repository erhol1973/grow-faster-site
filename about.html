How I approach a transformation question

  1. Start with an outcome that matters and can be assessed.
  2. Follow the complete workflow, including waiting, handoffs and rework.
  3. Clarify ownership and the decisions needed to improve it.
  4. Sequence changes to match dependencies and available capacity.
  5. Compare progress and outcomes with the starting point, and adjust.
