Connecting direction with execution

My background spans project management, PMO and business transformation. It shapes a perspective that connects strategic direction with the work people need to accomplish together, across organisational boundaries.

My focus is enterprise coherence: how strategy, governance, decisions, processes, data and learning reinforce one another. I examine AI through that lens, developing the technology and the organisation together.
