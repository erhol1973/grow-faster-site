## Search

Search normalizes case/accents and tokenizes words. AI and IA share one concept; one/two-character tokens require a whole-word match; longer terms support word prefixes. Multiple query tokens combine with AND. This preserves source/title, French claim and stable-ID prefix searches while avoiding AI inside `comptaient` and `factur` inside `Manufacturing`. Sector terms include NL/FR public labels and the existing full code. Blank search shows all scoped records; punctuation-only search has no matches. No AI backend or external search is introduced.
