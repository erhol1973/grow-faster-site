01. The GenAI paradox

AI Everywhere. Transformation Almost Nowhere.

Organizations have rapidly deployed GenAI, mostly as horizontal tools such as copilots and chatbots. These tools help with: Writing, Summarization, Information retrieval, Meeting preparation, Content generation and Individual productivity. The gains are real, but spread thinly across thousands of employees and individual tasks. The business process underneath stays largely the same.

The value gap. Adoption runs roughly fifteen times ahead of meaningful earnings impact. Only about 6% of organizations attribute 5% or more of their EBIT to AI.

Through the coherence lens

02. The coherence lens

Why AI Stalls in Fragmented Organizations

AI outcomes depend on both the technology and the organization around it. Waiting decisions, repeated handoffs and unclear responsibilities can limit the value of an otherwise useful application.

The AI Advantage Will Not Come From Having More AI.

It Will Come From Redesigning How Work Gets Done.

The organizations that create disproportionate value from AI will not simply deploy more copilots or build more agents. They will identify the processes that matter, redesign them around human and machine capabilities, govern agent autonomy, and connect AI investment directly to measurable business outcomes.

Five principles for coherent transformation

The Coherent Transformation Playbook · The Coherent Enterprise
  1. Protect absorption capacityChange only as fast as people and teams can absorb it.
  2. Reduce fragmentationFewer, connected initiatives instead of many parallel ones.
  3. Simplify governanceProportional controls, fewer layers, faster decisions.
  4. Protect operational continuityTransform without breaking what works today.
  5. Reduce friction before accelerationRemove waiting and handoffs before adding agents.
