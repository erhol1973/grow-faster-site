Where coherence becomes visible

Decisions keep waiting

Several people are involved, but it is unclear who can make the trade-off.

Work repeatedly changes hands

Each department completes its part while the shared outcome remains out of reach.

Priorities compete for the same capacity

Initiatives accumulate and ask for changes from the same people, systems and information at the same time.

I examine what happens between those parts. Which choices make sense separately but work poorly together? What needs a shared approach, and where does the organisation need room for differences?
