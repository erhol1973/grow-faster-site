The book behind this website

The Coherent Enterprise examines how strategic choices, governance, decisions, operations, technology and learning can reinforce one another as an organisation changes.

Jeanne Ross and her co-authors provide an important intellectual foundation, including their work on the operational backbone and digital platforms. My coherence perspective explores how these design questions connect with direction, transformation capacity and learning across the enterprise.

The AI perspective applies these questions to the connection between local productivity gains and lasting enterprise value.
