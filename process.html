The breakthrough does not come from automating every task. It comes from redesigning how the entire process creates value.

Through the coherence lens

You cannot redesign a process that nobody owns end-to-end. Before you draw the future workflow, name the person who owns the whole flow and the decisions inside it.

06. The economics of work

Why agents change the economics of work

Speed

Agents remove the waiting between tasks and execute work in parallel.

Adaptability

Processes respond to changing data and circumstances as they happen.

Personalization

Workflows adapt to the individual customer, employee or situation.

Elasticity

Digital capacity scales up and down with demand.

Resilience

Agents detect disruptions, reroute work and escalate exceptions.

Growth

Agents can support new services, products and revenue models.

Agentic AI is not only a productivity technology. It can become an operating-model technology.

Through the coherence lens

These gains only reach the customer when the surrounding system keeps pace. An agent that decides in seconds still waits days for a committee.
